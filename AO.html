Initiative Rules
Minimum 2 L1 Active in Sep 2026 required. For this count, Existing Active L1s + Recruited L1s are considered together - else cash payout is reduced by 50% and trips carry an additional ₹10,000 co-pay.
At ₹12L total WCP, the top slab pays ₹25,000. Every complete ₹2L beyond ₹12L adds another ₹25,000 (e.g. ₹15L = ₹25,000 + ₹25,000).
Trip encashment rates: Cordelia ₹45,000 and Bali ₹80,000.
Quality New L1 Recruitment
For each newly recruited L1 with ≥ ₹2L WCP in M0 → ₹2,500 per L1. If the L1 achieves Cordelia qualification (₹3.5L till 15 Sep / ₹5L till 30 Sep) → ₹6,000 per L1.
